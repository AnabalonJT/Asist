## Key Conventions

- **Backend routes** are prefixed with `/api/` (e.g., `/api/auth`, `/api/dashboard`)
- **One model per file** in `app/models/`, exported via `__init__.py`
- **One router per domain** in `app/routes/`
- **Services layer** handles business logic; routes are thin controllers
- **Frontend is self-contained** inside `src/` with its own `package.json`
- **In production**, the frontend is built to `static/` and served by FastAPI's StaticFiles mount with SPA fallback
- **Database sessions** use FastAPI's `Depends(get_db)` pattern with auto-commit/rollback
- **All DB operations are async** — use `await` with SQLAlchemy async session
